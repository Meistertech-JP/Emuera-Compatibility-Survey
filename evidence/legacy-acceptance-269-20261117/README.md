# Historical typed-source mapping: 269 targets

[日本語](README.ja.md)

This material records the source mappings identified in the historical audit. `layer.json` lists 269 typed targets whose current target row has no `current_acceptance` field. An absent field does not mean that acceptance material does not exist. The recorded finite-scope outcomes remain unchanged.

The historical audit identified a typed source for 205 targets (`TYPED_SOURCE_IDENTIFIED`) and did not identify one for 64 (`TYPED_SOURCE_NOT_IDENTIFIED`). The latter means unidentified by that audit, not proven absent. The former does not mean that an external reader has verified the document name, content, or sufficiency of the acceptance.

`history` identifies the audit creation time, audit-file SHA, and the target-review SHA captured at that time. For the 205, `historical_source_sha256` is the digest of the document identified in that historical audit; it is **not a current acceptance-field SHA**. The other 64 are `null`. Originals are not provided in this layer and have not been externally verified. Hashes alone do not establish content, identical conditions, observations, rights, or authenticity.

Paths in `candidate_snapshot` are relative to the root of the same distribution unit. Target pointers and case records apply only to the recorded snapshot hashes. The 37 case references distinguish direct cases from related context; consult the referenced records for shared executions, dead branches, and other limits. A referenced record does not make a withheld historical original externally verifiable.

Historical categories and gap codes describe **the historical audit**, not a determination that a gap persists today. Publication review is recorded as `PENDING` for every row. The content, appropriateness of disclosing source hashes, rights, privacy, external access to originals, and distribution terms require further review. No session transcript or personal path is included.
