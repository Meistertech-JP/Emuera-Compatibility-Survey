## Scope and interpretation

The package covers only the 29 recorded cases and their input and configuration conditions. The other 59 cases in the 88-case review are not included; omission does not mean they were unobserved. Two cases have different recorded values between Native and PyEmuera. `DONE` is a marker printed by a fixture; it does not establish natural application termination. This package makes no general compatibility, unrecorded-condition, or complete-GUI claim.

C005 is limited to the recorded empty-string/index-zero condition. C012 records a difference in Native/PyEmuera aggregate results. The statement that “value 9 was counted” is an inference from saved values and the aggregate result, not a direct observation of individual elements. See each case record for its conditions and limits.

## Reproduction and rights

Fixtures, configuration, CSV files, and saved raw logs are included. Engine executables, the complete execution environment, original receipts, and external source records are not included. A third party cannot independently recheck historical execution authenticity or the mapping to external records from this package alone, and the historical runs cannot be fully replayed. No screenshots are included, so GUI output cannot be compared. Follow-up runs are not guaranteed to produce the same results.

Each included notice and license applies to the scope it states. Their inclusion does not establish publication or redistribution rights for the package as a whole; conditions for some materials remain unconfirmed. See [the notice and license guide](notices/README.md).
