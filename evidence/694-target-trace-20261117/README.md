# 694 targets and their verification requirements

[日本語](README.ja.md)

This material records the relationships between targets and their verification requirements. `projection.json` identifies 694 targets, 53 typed evidence entries, 75 static API targets, and 24 separately counted cases. `requirements.jsonl` contains 3,359 source-attributed verification requirements: 3,278 verbatim and 81 neutralized. These retain the original requirements and do not constitute new execution instructions or measurements established by this material. References to recorded observations remain attributed to the original record.

Relative paths inside the `requirements` object are based at this directory; candidate `path` references are based at the root of the same distribution unit. JSON Pointers apply only to snapshots matching their recorded SHA values. Reconcile references if the referenced material changes. `case-id-crosswalk.json` maps 11 exactly identified case IDs in the text to aliases. Compound and abbreviated text remains intact; the precise membership of an abbreviated group is not inferred.

Original ledgers and private acceptance documents are not included. `WITHHELD_PRIVATE_SOURCE` is not a public link, and a candidate record's source ID or pointer does not make a withheld source readable. Text, hashes, and case references alone do not authenticate original material or establish observation, original receipts, fixture completeness, or identical execution conditions.

`WITHHELD_PRIVATE_SOURCE` means that the acceptance material is not included in this projection; it does not mean that the original material is missing or that the survey was incomplete. Each target's `survey_pointer` resolves to its recorded finite-scope classification and case IDs. For the 269 targets without a current acceptance-reference field, the [historical acceptance reference layer](../legacy-acceptance-269-20261117/README.md) distinguishes 205 targets with identified historical material from 64 whose material was not identified. This does not make the original documents public or independently reproduce the results.

The 53 rows distinguish 41 direct cases from 12 related contextual cases. The registered and neutral main29 IDs identify rows in a shared batch, not 29 separate runs. Two dead-branch cases did not enter the target command. PRINTDB15 is one physical case with two typed roles and three saved modes. Static API evidence contains no runtime raw; function-method dictionary registration does not prove command syntax or routing. The 16 grammar and eight supplemental cases remain separate from the main 694. Not every material was rehashed and the native engine was not rerun.

The requirement text, privacy and rights for evidence and materials require further review. Withheld source materials cannot be checked from this material alone, and distribution terms have not been determined. Hashes establish correspondence, not publisher authenticity.

Some requirement-case roles are labels derived by the index from the original ledger, case registry, and prior alias decisions. Their semantics have not been independently reaudited.
