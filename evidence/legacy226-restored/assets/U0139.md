## 観測

Native logged `ADDCHARA命令:引数が設定されていません` at line 4; public emitted `E_ARGUMENT` at the same ERB line on stderr. Neither reached AFTER or the completion marker. The Native repeat logs are byte-identical; both Native instances remained alive at five seconds and were cleaned up by the harness. Public exited 1 without creating emuera.log. This records a bounded diagnostic difference, not equivalent parser timing or full syntax behavior.
